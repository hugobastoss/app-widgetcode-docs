WidgetCode: Widgets for Flutter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

PT EN ES

This Privacy Policy describes how the WidgetCode: Widgets for Flutter app ("WidgetCode" or "app"), developed and maintained by HVCB App&Games ("developer", "we"), handles users' information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Summary: WidgetCode requires no sign-up, shows no ads and collects no personal data. The only information it keeps is your preferences (theme, language and favorite examples), and it stays only on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

1. Information kept on the device

To remember your choices, the app stores locally:

  • the chosen theme (light, dark or automatic);
  • the chosen language (Portuguese, English or Spanish);
  • the list of examples you marked as favorites (only the examples' identifiers, such as buttons/elevated_button_basico).

This information stays exclusively in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to third parties, and it does not identify you.

2. Internet access

The app has permission to access the internet, but it does not send any of your data. The connection is used only in these cases:

  • "Image.network" example: when you open this example, the device downloads a demo image from flutter.github.io, a public image repository of the Flutter documentation, hosted by GitHub. As with any website visit, that server receives technical connection data, such as the IP address, under GitHub's policies;
  • Links you tap: "Open on GitHub", "Open the repository", "Rate the app" and "More apps" open GitHub or Google Play pages in the browser or in the corresponding app. From there on, those services' privacy policies apply.

3. Sharing on your initiative

  • Copy: the "Copy code", "Ask your AI" and "Ask your AI for all" buttons copy text to the device's clipboard. The app does not send that text anywhere;
  • Refer to friends: builds a message with the app's link and opens Android's own share sheet. You choose the recipient and send it;
  • AI tools: if you paste a request into a coding AI (such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot), whatever is sent to it follows that service's rules and privacy policy, which are unrelated to WidgetCode.

4. Support

The "Contact support" button, on the About the app screen, opens your email app with a message to hvcbappegames@gmail.com, with the subject and the app version already filled in. The message is only sent if you choose to send it, from your own email account. We use what you write, your email address and the app version only to reply to you, and we do not share this information with third parties.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not require sign-up, an account or login;
  • We do not show ads or use advertising SDKs;
  • We do not use behavior analytics or crash reporting tools;
  • We do not sell, rent or share data;
  • We do not access the device's contacts, camera, microphone, files or location.

6. Data retention and deletion

Preferences stay on the device until you change them in the app, clear its data in Android's settings or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes everything the app stored.

Messages sent to support by email stay in our mailbox for as long as they are needed to help you. You can ask us to delete them at any time, at the same address.

7. Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, LGPD) and other applicable laws, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data. Since the app collects no personal data and preferences stay on your own device, under your control, you can change or delete them yourself at any time. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at the email at the end of this page.

8. Children

The app is a study tool for people who build apps and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

Preferences stay in the app's private area on the device and nothing is sent to our servers, so there is no WidgetCode database holding users' information. Even so, we recommend keeping your device's system up to date.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app or in legal requirements. The current version will always be available on this page, with the update date at the top. Relevant changes will be highlighted in the app's update notes on the Play Store.
